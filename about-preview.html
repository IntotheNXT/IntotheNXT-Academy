What we believe

Better performance starts with better conditions for people.

People do their best work when they understand what matters, have the autonomy and capability to act, keep learning, collaborate well and can access the knowledge they need.

Good leadership and management create those conditions deliberately, so entrepreneurship and innovation become part of everyday work rather than isolated initiatives.

The impact we want to make
Better companies.
Better work.
Better outcomes.

Our ambition is to help transform a meaningful number of organisations into companies that perform better and are genuinely better places to work.

The legacy we want to create is not a large consultancy for its own sake. It is a body of practical capability that helps organisations become more entrepreneurial, innovative and effective, while leaving behind capabilities that continue to create value after we are gone.

Where this came from

A short personal starting point.

After working in consulting and then building startups, I became increasingly interested in why some organisations create energy, ownership, learning and innovation while others unintentionally suppress them. That brought me back to leadership and management itself, and ultimately to the question IntotheNXT is built around: how do we design organisations so people can perform at their best and create more value?

Our values

How we try to work.

These values shape how we work with clients, partners and each other. They are practical reminders of the conditions we believe help people perform, learn and create value.

01

Drive

Purpose, autonomy, mastery and belonging create the conditions for people to take ownership and do their best work.
